    In English

    Hataktzir is an AI system that shortens long-form video into ready-to-publish clips, with no manual editing. It started with 12-hour livestreams on YouTube, the niche I come from. It detects when a stream ends, transcribes it on a home GPU, and an LLM reads the whole transcript to pick and score the moments worth watching. The system then cuts them, writes titles and descriptions, picks a thumbnail frame with a vision model, credits the creator and uploads. A human approves with one tap.

    Measured on a real 11.7-hour stream: $1.84 of model cost, about 5 hours from stream end to the first clip online, all on one PC with a GTX 1650. Israel is the test market; the pipeline is language-agnostic, and the same system fits anyone who films long-form video: lecturers, podcasters, fitness coaches. English (2-3x the RPM) and creators abroad are next. The next infrastructure step is a cloud GPU server, so several streams can be processed in parallel instead of on one home PC.
